What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and understanding language.

How AI Is Used in Everyday Life

Here are three examples of AI applications:

  • Virtual assistants help people answer questions and complete tasks.
  • Recommendation systems suggest movies, music, and products.
  • Self-driving technology helps vehicles understand their surroundings.

My Learning Goals

I want to learn programming, understand machine learning, and build useful AI applications that solve real-world problems.
